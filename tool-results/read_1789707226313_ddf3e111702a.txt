  4858→## §37 Lemon Squeezy Integration Requirements
  4859→
  4860→### 37.1 Lemon Squeezy Concepts
  4861→
  4862→Understanding Lemon Squeezy's data model is essential for correct integration:
  4863→
  4864→| Concept | Description |
  4865→|---|---|
  4866→| **Store** | The MemorableDay Lemon Squeezy store |
  4867→| **Product** | A plan (e.g., "Personal Plan") |
  4868→| **Variant** | A billing period for a product (e.g., "Personal Monthly", "Personal Annual") |
  4869→| **Customer** | A Lemon Squeezy customer record (linked to MemorableDay user) |
  4870→| **Subscription** | An active subscription to a variant |
  4871→| **Order** | A completed purchase (subscription or one-time) |
  4872→| **Checkout** | The hosted checkout page for a variant |
  4873→| **Webhook** | Event notification from Lemon Squeezy to MemorableDay |
  4874→
  4875→### 37.2 Product and Variant Structure
  4876→
  4877→Each MemorableDay plan is a Lemon Squeezy Product with two Variants (monthly and annual):
  4878→
  4879→| Plan | Product | Variants |
  4880→|---|---|---|
  4881→| Personal | Personal Plan | Personal Monthly, Personal Annual |
  4882→| Pro | Pro Plan | Pro Monthly, Pro Annual |
  4883→| Business | Business Plan | Business Monthly, Business Annual |
  4884→| Growth | Growth Plan | Growth Monthly, Growth Annual |
  4885→| Agency | Agency Plan | Agency Monthly, Agency Annual |
  4886→| Enterprise | Enterprise Plan | Enterprise Annual (custom) |
  4887→
  4888→**Requirements:**
  4889→- `PRD-PAY-004`: Each plan must have a corresponding Lemon Squeezy Product
  4890→- `PRD-PAY-005`: Each billing period must be a separate Lemon Squeezy Variant
  4891→- `PRD-PAY-006`: Variant IDs must be stored in MemorableDay's configuration for entitlement mapping
  4892→
  4893→### 37.3 Checkout Flow
  4894→
  4895→**Checkout flow:**
  4896→
  4897→1. User selects a plan in MemorableDay
  4898→2. MemorableDay generates a Lemon Squeezy checkout URL for the selected variant
  4899→3. User is redirected to Lemon Squeezy's hosted checkout
  4900→4. User completes payment on Lemon Squeezy
  4901→5. Lemon Squeezy sends `order_created` and `subscription_created` webhooks to MemorableDay
  4902→6. MemorableDay updates the user's subscription status and entitlements
  4903→7. User is redirected back to MemorableDay with a success message
  4904→
  4905→**Requirements:**
  4906→- `PRD-PAY-007`: Checkout URL must include the user's email (pre-filled) and a custom redirect URL
  4907→- `PRD-PAY-008`: Checkout must include a `custom_data` parameter with the MemorableDay user ID for webhook correlation
  4908→- `PRD-PAY-009`: Checkout redirect must return the user to the correct page after payment
  4909→- `PRD-PAY-010`: Checkout must support discount codes (for promotions)
  4910→
  4911→### 37.4 Webhook Events
  4912→
  4913→MemorableDay must handle the following Lemon Squeezy webhook events:
  4914→
  4915→| Event | Description | MemorableDay Action |
  4916→|---|---|---|
  4917→| `order_created` | New order placed | Record order, update subscription status |
  4918→| `subscription_created` | New subscription created | Activate plan, grant entitlements |
  4919→| `subscription_updated` | Subscription changed (upgrade/downgrade) | Update plan, adjust entitlements |
  4920→| `subscription_cancelled` | Subscription cancelled | Schedule plan downgrade at period end |
  4921→| `subscription_resumed` | Cancelled subscription resumed | Reactivate plan |
  4922→| `subscription_expired` | Subscription expired (after cancellation) | Downgrade to free plan |
  4923→| `subscription_paused` | Subscription paused | Restrict access (maintain data) |
  4924→| `subscription_unpaused` | Subscription unpaused | Restore access |
  4925→| `subscription_payment_success` | Renewal payment succeeded | Update renewal date |
  4926→| `subscription_payment_failed` | Renewal payment failed | Send dunning email, restrict access after grace period |
  4927→| `subscription_payment_recovered` | Failed payment recovered | Restore full access |
  4928→| `subscription_payment_refunded` | Payment refunded | Adjust subscription status |
  4929→
  4930→**Requirements:**
  4931→- `PRD-PAY-011`: All Lemon Squeezy webhooks must be verified using the webhook signature
  4932→- `PRD-PAY-012`: Webhook processing must be idempotent (duplicate events must not cause duplicate actions)
  4933→- `PRD-PAY-013`: Webhook processing must be asynchronous (respond 200 immediately, process in background)
  4934→- `PRD-PAY-014`: Failed webhook processing must be retried with exponential backoff
  4935→- `PRD-PAY-015`: Webhook events must be logged for debugging and audit
  4936→
  4937→### 37.5 Subscription Lifecycle Management
  4938→
  4939→**Upgrade flow:**
  4940→1. User selects a higher plan
  4941→2. MemorableDay redirects to Lemon Squeezy checkout for the new variant
  4942→3. Lemon Squeezy handles proration (charges the difference)
  4943→4. `subscription_updated` webhook received
  4944→5. MemorableDay immediately grants new entitlements
  4945→
  4946→**Downgrade flow:**
  4947→1. User selects a lower plan
  4948→2. MemorableDay redirects to Lemon Squeezy checkout or customer portal
  4949→3. Lemon Squeezy schedules the downgrade at the end of the current period
  4950→4. `subscription_updated` webhook received (with `ends_at` date)
  4951→5. MemorableDay maintains current entitlements until `ends_at`
  4952→6. At `ends_at`: `subscription_expired` or `subscription_updated` webhook received
  4953→7. MemorableDay applies new (lower) entitlements
  4954→
  4955→**Cancellation flow:**
  4956→1. User cancels via Lemon Squeezy customer portal or MemorableDay UI
  4957→2. `subscription_cancelled` webhook received
  4958→3. MemorableDay maintains current entitlements until end of billing period
  4959→4. At period end: `subscription_expired` webhook received
  4960→5. MemorableDay downgrades to free plan
  4961→
  4962→**Failed payment flow:**
  4963→1. Renewal payment fails
  4964→2. `subscription_payment_failed` webhook received
  4965→3. MemorableDay sends dunning email to user
  4966→4. Lemon Squeezy retries payment (per its dunning schedule)
  4967→5. If payment recovered: `subscription_payment_recovered` webhook → restore access
  4968→6. If payment not recovered after grace period: `subscription_expired` webhook → downgrade to free
  4969→
  4970→**Requirements:**
  4971→- `PRD-PAY-016`: Users must not lose access immediately on payment failure (grace period of at least 3 days)
  4972→- `PRD-PAY-017`: Users must receive email notifications for: payment failure, upcoming renewal, cancellation confirmation
  4973→- `PRD-PAY-018`: Users must be able to update payment method via Lemon Squeezy customer portal
  4974→
  4975→### 37.6 Customer Portal
  4976→
  4977→Lemon Squeezy provides a hosted customer portal where users can:
  4978→- View subscription details
  4979→- Update payment method
  4980→- Download invoices
  4981→- Cancel subscription

  4986→- `PRD-PAY-020`: Customer portal must be accessible without re-entering payment details
  4987→
  4988→### 37.7 Free Trials

  4992→- `PRD-PAY-022`: Trial period must be configurable per plan (recommended: 14 days for Business plans)

  5285→## §40 Free Plan
  5286→
  5287→### 40.1 Free Plan Philosophy
  5288→
  5289→The free plan is a strategic product decision, not a charity. It must:
  5290→
  5291→1. **Drive acquisition** — give users a reason to sign up without a credit card
  5292→2. **Drive viral growth** — free users create experiences that recipients see
  5293→3. **Drive conversion** — free users must hit limits that make upgrading obvious
  5294→4. **Not be economically unsustainable** — free users must not cost more than they're worth
  5295→
  5296→**The free plan is not:**
  5297→- A full-featured product (that would eliminate the upgrade incentive)
  5298→- A crippled product (that would drive users away)
  5299→- A marketing trick (that would damage trust)
  5300→
  5301→### 40.2 Free Plan Features

  5303→| Feature | Free Plan |
  5304→|---|---|
  5305→| Published experiences | 3 (lifetime, not monthly) |

  5323→
  5324→### 40.3 Free Plan Limits Rationale
  5325→
  5326→**Why 3 published experiences (not monthly)?**
  5327→- Creates urgency to upgrade without being too restrictive
  5328→- 3 experiences is enough to experience the product's value
  5329→- Lifetime limit (not monthly) means free users don't get unlimited experiences by waiting
  5330→
  5331→**Why 5 AI credits/month?**
  5332→- Enough to experience AI value (1–2 complete experiences)
  5333→- Not enough to rely on AI without upgrading
  5334→- Resets monthly to keep users engaged
  5335→
  5336→**Why no scheduling?**
  5337→- Scheduling is a key value driver for Personal plan
  5338→- Removing it creates a clear upgrade reason for occasion-based users
  5339→
  5340→**Why no video upload?**
  5341→- Video is a significant storage cost driver
  5342→- Removing it keeps free plan infrastructure costs low
  5343→
  5344→### 40.4 Free Plan Conversion Strategy
  5345→
  5346→**Conversion triggers (moments when free users are most likely to upgrade):**
  5347→
  5348→| Trigger | Upgrade Prompt |
  5349→|---|---|
  5350→| Creating 3rd experience | "You've used all 3 free experiences. Upgrade to Personal for unlimited experiences." |
  5351→| Trying to schedule | "Scheduling is available on Personal plan. Upgrade to schedule this experience." |
  5352→| Trying to upload video | "Video upload is available on Personal plan. Upgrade to add video." |
  5353→| Running out of AI credits | "You've used all 5 AI credits. Upgrade for 30 credits/month." |
  5354→| Trying to remove branding | "Remove MemorableDay branding on Pro plan." |
  5355→| Recipient opens experience | "Your experience was opened! Upgrade to see detailed analytics." |
  5356→
  5357→**Requirements:**
  5358→- `PRD-UX-034`: Conversion prompts must be contextual and specific
  5359→- `PRD-UX-035`: Conversion prompts must show the exact plan that unlocks the feature
  5360→- `PRD-UX-036`: Conversion prompts must include a direct checkout link
  5361→- `PRD-UX-037`: Free users must be able to see what they're missing (feature preview with lock icon)
  5362→
  5363→### 40.5 Free Plan Infrastructure Cost
  5364→
  5365→**Estimated cost per free user per month:**
  5366→- Storage (100MB): ~$0.002 (Cloudflare R2)
  5367→- CDN bandwidth (500 views × ~1MB): ~$0.05 (Cloudflare CDN)
  5368→- AI credits (5 × ~$0.01): ~$0.05
  5369→- Compute (Cloudflare Workers): ~$0.01
  5370→- **Total: ~$0.11/month per free user**
  5371→
  5372→At 100,000 free users: ~$11,000/month in infrastructure costs.
  5373→
  5374→This is acceptable if free-to-paid conversion rate is ≥ 3% (3,000 paying users × $9/month = $27,000 MRR).
  5375→
  5376→**Requirements:**
  5377→- `PRD-INFRA-001`: Free plan infrastructure costs must be monitored monthly
  5378→- `PRD-INFRA-002`: If free plan costs exceed 20% of MRR, free plan limits must be reviewed

  5380→### 40.6 Free Plan Abuse Prevention
  5381→

  5383→- `PRD-SEC-025`: Free plan must require email verification before creating experiences
  5384→- `PRD-SEC-026`: Free plan must have rate limiting on experience creation (max 3 per day)
